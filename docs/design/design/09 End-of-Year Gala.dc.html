Alẹ́ náà•The evening

The evening

The Gala closes our year. It is the night the community dresses, sits down together, and pays for the work of the next twelve months: the language lessons, the children's programs, and a festival that stays free because this evening is not.

18:00
Reception

Arrival, greeting, and photographs in the foyer.

19:30
Dinner

Seated, by table. Yoruba menu with a vegetarian option.

20:30
Recognitions

Three honorees, ten minutes each.

21:00
Performance

Bàtá drummers and a guest artist, announced in October.

21:45
Dancing

Until midnight.

The running order is confirmed in October and posted here.
