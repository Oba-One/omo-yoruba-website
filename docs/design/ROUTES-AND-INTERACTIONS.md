(fourteen interactions, four patterns). Source for page structure:
`design/06 Site Wireframes.dc.html` and each page's `.dc.html`.

## 1. Routes

All public routes are server-rendered and cached (`Astro.cache`, `s-maxage`
1 day, `stale-while-revalidate` 7 days, purged by the Sanity webhook).
Preview, Studio, API and action routes are never cached.

## 2. Site chrome (every page)

- **Nav** (`SiteNav.astro`): sticky, white at 97% with a hairline. Logo mark
  40px plus wordmark on two lines (second line hidden under 1060px). Links:
  Events (dropdown: Ọdúndé Festival, End-of-Year Gala), Programs, Get Involved,
  Impact, Our Story. Gold Donate button (small). Current page: terracotta text
  with a 2px gold underline. Dropdown opens on hover and focus-within, closes on
  Escape. Under 760px: burger opens a full-screen indigo overlay with the same
  links flattened (Events as a group label), close ×, Donate at the bottom.
  Focus trapped while open. Anchors get `scroll-margin-top: 96px`.
- **Footer** (`SiteFooter.astro`): indigo-900, àdìrẹ dot field at 13%, four
  columns (about, programs, events, get involved), newsletter form ("Festival
  news and updates, in your inbox"), trust line "501(c)(3) nonprofit since 1997 •
  EIN XX-XXXXXXX • Los Angeles, CA", mailing address, Contact link, social chips
  (IG, FB, LI, YT text initials in circles), links shift to gold-300 on hover.
- **Give Dialog** mounted once in the layout. **Enquiry Modal** mounted once,
  opened with a `kind`. Both are `<dialog>` elements with focus trap, Escape,
  scrim click to close, and focus return to the trigger.
- **Cross-fade** between pages via `<ClientRouter>`, 380ms, off under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`. A 2px gold progress bar at the top while loading.
- **PostHog** loaded after first paint. Capture pageviews and these events:
  `enquiry_opened` (kind), `enquiry_submitted` (kind), `give_opened`,
  `give_embed_failed`, `eventbrite_out`, `newsletter_submitted`,
  `lightbox_opened`. No autocapture of form field contents.

## 3. The fourteen interactions

The rule the design applied: **inline** when the form is long, carries an upload
or fee, or needs the page as context; **modal** when short and triggered from many
places; **handoff** when money moves, our page covers the decision and the
platform covers the card; **page** for browsing. Later polish moved every form
into the Enquiry Modal, opened from a card that explains what it asks first.

### Forms we own (write to Sanity `enquiry`, then email)

| Kind | Title | Triggered from | Fields (label, required) | Success copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `member` | Become a member | Nav, door cards, homepage, take-part bands | Full name*, Email*, Phone, Household or individual (select), Hometown association (select, optional), How did you hear (select), Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ Welcome." then: dues are not paid here; someone writes within five working days |
| `volunteer` | Volunteer with us | Footer, take-part band on five pages, festival | Full name*, Email*, Phone, When are you free (select), What can you do (select), Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓" then: the volunteer coordinator writes within a week |
| `enrol` | Write to the teacher | Lessons header, programs hub, homepage | Learner name*, Age*, Your name*, Email*, Level guess (select), When suits you (select), Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ Your message is with the teacher." then: she replies with a level, a time, and the call link; fees agreed with her |
| `vendor` | Apply for a booth at Ọjà Balógun | Festival header, take-part band, Get Involved signpost | Business name*, Contact name*, Email*, Phone, What you sell (select), Booth size (select), Website or Instagram, Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ Your application is in." then: reviewed in order received; a booth is held once the fee is paid |
| `contact` | Send us a message | Footer, Our Story "Reach us", Get Involved fallback | Name*, Email*, Subject (select), Message* (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ We have your message." then: someone replies within three working days; phone for urgent |
| `sponsor` | Sponsor Omo Yorùbá | Odunde, Gala, Impact, Get Involved, homepage partner door | Organization*, Your name and role*, Email*, Interest (select: Odunde, Gala, Collective, all), Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ The deck is on its way." then: partnerships lead sends it within one working day |
| `performer` | Ask about performing | Festival take-part band | Group or artist name*, Contact name*, Email*, Kind of performance (select), Link to a video, Anything else (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ We have your details." then: the program is decided in the spring; you hear either way |
| `table` | Reserve a table of ten | Gala table tier card | Your name*, Organization, Email*, Phone, Guests known so far (area) | "Ẹ ṣé! ✓ Your table is held." then: invoice by email within two working days |
| `newsletter` | (footer form) | Every page footer, homepage band variant | Email* | Button label flips to "Ẽ ṣé! ✓" in place |

Exact field lists, select options, and copy live in `design/Enquiry Modal.dc.html`
(`FORMS` object). Port them into one spec file in `@oy/content`
(`enquiry-kinds.ts`) that drives the Studio schema, the Zod action schema, the
Astro form markup, and the Storybook stories. One source, four consumers.

Named contacts in the confirmation copy (Folasade Adeyemi, Tunde Bakare,
Adebayo Ogunlesi, Yetunde Ojo) are **mock**. Make them fields on `siteSettings`
(`contacts[]` with role and name) and render "our membership lead" when empty.

Form states to build and test: empty, filled, submitting ("Sending..." and
disabled), success (Ẹ ṣé! ✓ block replaces the fields, close button focused),
error (summary sentence at the top with `role="alert"`, each field named, values
kept). Human fallback line under every form: email and phone from `siteSettings`.

### Money handoffs

- **Donate**: every Donate button opens the Give Dialog. It wraps Zeffy's embed
  in our card (their embed shows amount and payment only; their API is read-only).
  If the iframe fails to load within 4s, show the fallback: "Give by check" with
  the mailing address, and a Contact button. `#give` in any URL opens the dialog
  on load. Capture `give_opened`.
- **Gala seats**: "Get tickets" opens Eventbrite in a new tab (`rel="noopener"`),
  with a one-line notice on the card. Until the URL exists, the button points at
  the tiers block and shows a Pending chip in Studio's dashboard.
- **Gala tables**: enquiry modal kind `table`, invoiced by hand. (Open question 4.)

### Browsing

- **Gallery**: album tile opens `/gallery/[album]`. A photo opens the lightbox
  overlay (`<dialog>`), writes `?photo=<key>` to the URL with `history.pushState`,
  Back closes it, arrows and swipe move, Escape closes, focus returns to the
  tile. Caption and credit inside the overlay. Captions on tiles: always on or
  on hover (Studio option).
- **Filtering** (news, later): chip row plus year select, updates the URL query
  in place, never reloads, announces the result count.
- **Mobile nav**: full-screen overlay, described above.

### Shared conventions (all fourteen)

- Success in Yoruba, next steps in English.
- Errors are sentences, never colour alone, never clear input.
- Every form shows a human fallback beside it.
- Nothing opens on load except `#give`.
- Focus is trapped in dialogs, returns to the trigger on close, Escape closes.
- Under 720px dialogs become bottom sheets with a drag handle look (no drag
  behaviour required).

## 4. Page-by-page blocks

Each page's section order is the prototype's section order. Read the `.dc.html`
for exact copy and layout. The kicker pattern is Yoruba first, then "•", then
English, uppercase 12px tracked.

- **Homepage**: hero (photo, scrim, kicker "Ẹ káàbọ̀ • Welcome", H1 "Celebrating
  and sustaining Yoruba culture in Southern California since 1997", sub, two
  buttons), event band (next event), stat strip (29 years, 3,000+ community, 5
  festival zones, 9 hometown associations), programs (4 cards), member voices,
  news & events (3), a year in the life (photo tiles 3/5/7), raise your hand
  (doors or rows), newsletter (footer by default, band variant), footer.
- **Odunde**: photo band header, at a glance (5 facts), what Ọdúndé is (with the
  Chinese New Year / Diwali / Nowruz framing), zones mosaic (4 zones today),
  the day hour by hour (schedule rows, hideable), plan your visit (8 facts),
  take part (vendor first), past years (framed carousel), partners.
- **Gala**: photo band header, at a glance, the evening (running order,
  hideable), seats and tables (tier cards, buy-now and enquiry variants),
  sponsor the Gala (one gold action), honourees (hideable), past galas
  (carousel), take part.
- **Programs**: slim header, four program cards (or three, or pairs), Kids &
  STEM inline, Cultural Exchange inline, when things run (year strip), take part.
- **Yoruba Language Lessons**: slim header, at a glance (online, one teacher,
  ages, write to enrol), the teacher (person card, portrait optional), what you
  learn (three levels), what a lesson looks like (hideable), questions parents
  ask (FAQ, five, single open), take part (enrol, volunteer, give).
- **Cultural Collective**: slim header with green signal, why culture and
  sustainability sit together, collective events (CMS, hideable), Solar Hub and
  Green Goods (side by side or stacked, member-led label, status line hideable),
  one voice, build with the Collective.
- **Get Involved**: slim header, four doors (cards or rows) each opening its
  modal kind, hometown associations (prose, hideable), or just talk to someone.
- **Impact**: slim header, headline numbers (4 or 6, each with a source line),
  how we work, what each program produced (outcome cards or rows), Ọdúndé as
  civic infrastructure, in their words, the work in photographs, governance and
  accountability (990, annual report, audit position), partners and funders
  (hideable), fund the next year.
- **Our Story**: slim header, how it began 1997, 1997 to now (timeline,
  hideable), board of directors (person cards, portraits optional, bios short or
  full), staff and volunteers, reach us (contact block, opens `contact`), take part.
- **Donate**: slim header, give now (single gold button opens Give Dialog),
  giving at a larger scale (partner and sponsor doors), what your gift does
  (hideable), other ways to give, tax-deductible line.
- **Gallery**: kicker "Àwòrán", album mosaic, per-album page with the grid and
  viewer, photography credit and permissions.
