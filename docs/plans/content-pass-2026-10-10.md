# Content pass, 10 October 2026

What the live site showed on 10 October 2026, read for the organization's meeting in the week of 12 October:
the Pending chips on its pages, so each can be filled or accepted as visible, and what looked wrong on a
phone or a laptop. The four-week plan's meeting pack ("What we need from you") draws on this list.

**How it was read.** A script opened the 14 public pages of `https://www.omoyorubasocal.org` at 375 and 1440
wide, scrolled each to its end, and recorded the chips, any sideways overflow, any image or request that
failed, and any console error. It also asked once, at 1440, for a page that does not exist. A reader then went
through full-page screenshots of every page at both widths. Not covered: the eight enquiry forms and the lines
beside them, which a page draws only once a form is opened; draft mode; the Studio.

## What held

- All 14 pages answer 200 at both widths, each with one h1, a title and a description; the not-found page
  answers 404 in the site's chrome.
- No page scrolls sideways, no image or request failed, and the console stayed clean.
- The Yoruba marks render cleanly everywhere, and no placeholder text is left in.
