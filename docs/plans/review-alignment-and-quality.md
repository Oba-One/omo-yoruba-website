# Deep review: design alignment and code quality

Run on 27 September 2026 as part 3 of the weeks 1 and 2 plan, by `docs/plans/prompt-deep-review.md`. It reviews and
does not fix: every finding below has its ticket under `docs/tickets/review/issues/`, for the owner to triage. Nothing
in this pull request changes the site.

## Summary

Three blockers, all small to fix, and two of them are security:

- **R03, drafts are readable by anyone.** The draft-mode cookie is not signed, so a visitor who sets it by hand on
  any address the CDN has not cached reads unpublished drafts: editions prepared for their announce day, draft news,
  and album photographs that may still wait on consent. The preview secret gates nothing.
- **R02, a `javascript:` link would run from every page.** The footer's social links skip the link check every other
  Studio link passes through; an Editor can write site settings through the API (ADR 0042), and the CSP is still
  report-only.
- **R01, the consent hold hides almost nothing.** The gallery's Coming soon switch, which the Studio and D5 describe
  as the hold while photo consent is settled, only replaces `/gallery`'s mosaic. Every album page, every shared
  photo address and the event pages' past years keep the photographs public. The owner decides what the hold should
  withdraw.

Nineteen majors, in five groups:

- **Money and honesty.** Monthly giving and email receipts are stated as fact on Donate, the Gala and in the Give
  Dialog, while the registry holds them as owed, and the dialog says "Powered by Zeffy" before any form exists (R38).
  The Give Dialog tells a donor to send a check to the organization's name with no address and no Pending chip (R45).
- **Forms and dialogs.**
  - After one page change, every Donate and form trigger also navigates the page underneath (R44). Pull request 10
    fixes this; it was not in this stack when the review ran and has merged since (see the triage below).
  - Without JavaScript the Enquiry Modal cannot be closed (R46).
  - A stray drag onto the scrim closes a form and loses what was typed (R47).
  - Back after closing a dialog opened by its address changes the address but not the page (R109).
  - Form fields are 15px, so iPhone Safari zooms on every field (R49).
- **Contrast under AA.** Quiet links on the indigo tint on hover (R37), album captions over bright photographs (R42),
  the footer's newsletter line on every page (R50), and the Odunde header kicker at 375 (R12).
- **Latent layouts and the To do.**
  - The Gala's sponsor levels squeeze into a 72px column on phones (R15).
  - The Lessons teacher card breaks once she has a portrait (R21).
  - The To do stops asking for voices and outcomes after the first, while the pages still show Pending slots
    (R85). Members start from the To do after the meeting.
- **Engineering safety.**
  - TypeGen drift and the Playwright job are not required checks (R142).
  - The weekly dependency audit has failed on every run since it was wired, unrecorded (R143).
  - The git guardrail hook misses common spellings of the commands it claims to block (R144).
  - Storybook's card stories render outside the page root, so they show the wrong card design, and the Chromatic
    baselines would capture it (R48).
  - The 17px body floor is not met by most secondary running text, which needs a decision on what it covers (R110).

In all, 165 findings: 3 blockers, 19 major, 81 minor and 62 polish. 21 need an owner decision. Design alignment holds
up well: section rhythm, heading sizes, the card language, hover, the nav's current page, the one gold action on
every page but the homepage, and the register's honesty all check out (see "Checked and found clean"). Most design
drift is polish, and much of the rest is latent: it appears only once owed content arrives, and each such finding
says so.

  build pass; Playwright with `--workers=1` seeded 285 passed and 11 skipped, placeholder 256 passed and 40 skipped.
  No failure, so no baseline blocker.

  - Design, seven: the homepage and event pages, the program pages, the trust pages, the gallery with the Lightbox

  followed by reading the command and its vocabulary skill. Its candidates are below; run the command for its HTML
  report and the walkthrough of a chosen candidate.
- **Triage.** The reviewers reported 204 findings. 39 were duplicates, the same root cause found by two to six
  reviewers, and merged into one finding each with every reviewer's evidence. Three were raised to blocker, and one

## Architecture candidates

The history's hot spots: the Pending registry (`packages/content/src/pending.ts`, 37 of the last 150 commits, its test
29), the TypeGen output (36 and 24), the page singletons (25), the seed (24, and 19 of those commits also edited a
builder test or a UI fixture that copies it), the shared document types (18) and the route map (17, with two
omissions caught only in review, b26e2ed and eb52ce4).

1. **The Pending registry decides each chip, not only its wording** (Strong; keeps ADR 0014 and makes it
   enforceable).
   - Today the To do checks each row with GROQ, while eleven view builders and twelve components decide emptiness
     again in TypeScript, then look up the wording through a regular-expression match over the GROQ text. 66 of the
     91 lookups repeat the wording as a fallback, and ten constants are a second way to the same words.
   - Two disagreements exist already (in R104's evidence).
   - The deepened module answers the site's question and the To do's from one rule per row. A parity test per page
     on the seed, through both queries with groq-js, is the first step and changes no behaviour.
2. **A route's read: its query, view and type tags in one module, tested on the seed** (Strong; keeps ADR 0021).
   - What a route reads is kept in three places by hand: the query, `TYPE_ROUTES`, and the tags, purge and
     Presentation locations that come from it.
   - Builder tests run on hand copies of the seed with 65 type casts.
   - Deriving or checking the types from the query, and running builders on the real query over the seed, removes
     both. It shares the parity harness of candidate 1.
3. **One page body per page, shared by the site and its layout-option stories** (Worth exploring; reopens the
   working rule that the page file owns the copy, so it is the owner's call).
   - Each page is composed twice, in the site page and in `ui/src/pages/*/sections.ts` (2216 lines), with 95
     kickers, headings and intros written in both.
   - 34 of the 36 commits to a `sections.ts` also changed its site page.
4. **One routing contact decision per enquiry kind** (Worth exploring; keeps ADR 0016).
   - The email routing and the success copy fall back differently.
   - A contact with a name and no email is promised to reply while the email went elsewhere (in R91's evidence).
5. **One cache decision per response** (Worth exploring; keeps ADR 0021). Five modules decide whether a response may
   be cached, and the outcome depends on their order. The middleware, which already runs last, could decide once from
   what the loader noted.

**Top recommendation:** the registry first. It is the busiest module, it carries the site's honesty rule, members
start from the To do after the meeting, and its first step, the parity harness, also serves candidate 2.
