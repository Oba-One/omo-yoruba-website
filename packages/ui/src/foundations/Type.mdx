# Type

Source Serif 4 carries the headings (600 and 700) and Source Sans 3 carries body and UI text
(400, 600 and 700), falling back through Noto Serif and Noto Sans, then Georgia and system-ui.
Both faces are self-hosted from `@oy/tokens/fonts.css` in three subsets (latin, latin-ext and
vietnamese), the minimum that covers Yoruba. Every size below must show the test string with no
tofu and every mark in place: the underdots on Ẹ, Ọ and ṣ, and the tone marks that sit on ẹ́ and
ẹ̀ and ọ̀. Sizes come from the type tokens: hero 44 to 64px, H2 32 to 40, H3 20 to 24, body 17,
caption 13.5, kicker 12 uppercase.
